## How to book Mogao Caves tickets with a foreign passport

The [Dunhuang Academy’s 2026 booking notice](https://www.dha.ac.cn/info/1020/7498.htm) gives foreign passport holders a route to reserve a timed visit in advance.

1. In WeChat, find the official service account **莫高窟参观预约网** (Mogao Caves Visit Reservation).
2. Open **预约购票** and choose **Ticket Booking**. Use the passport details of each visitor.
3. Save your booking and bring the original passport used to reserve. Allow time to reach the **Mogao Caves Digital Exhibition Center** before your session.

Check the Academy’s notice for your visit date. If a regular slot is sold out or the passport booking option fails, ask the official ticket office about the dates and ticket types available for your passport before you make the trip. The phone line listed in the notice provides advice.

## Where to go on the day

Save **莫高窟数字展示中心** as your drop-off point. It is the Mogao Caves Digital Exhibition Center at **8899 Yangguan East Road (阳关东路8899号)**. The [Academy’s visitor directions](https://www.dha.ac.cn/skxl/mgk.htm) route visitors through the center and then onward to the caves by the site shuttle.

The [2026 notice](https://www.dha.ac.cn/info/1020/7498.htm) asks visitors to arrive at the center **30 minutes before the reserved time**. For a regular visit, collect the paper booking slip at the center and keep it for the checks there and at the caves. Follow the entrance listed on the slip. For planning, set aside a half day so check-in, the shuttle and the guided visit fit without a tight connection. Use our [two-day Dunhuang itinerary](/places/dunhuang/two-day-itinerary/) to put the dunes on a separate day.

## Are English-language tours available?

The [Dunhuang Academy lists English among its guided interpretation services](https://www.dha.ac.cn/jggk/jgsz/ywbm/whhyb1.htm). Ask the operator whether English interpretation is available for your date and ticket type, and where to meet the guide. Check this when you book so you can leave time for the visit in your itinerary.

## Regular, emergency and special cave visits

The [Academy’s 2026 notice](https://www.dha.ac.cn/info/1020/7498.htm) distinguishes three visit types. Check what is offered for your date.

- **Regular visit.** Start with the digital presentation and continue to the guided cave visit.
- **Emergency visit.** The Academy may open a shorter visit format when demand is high. Check whether it is available for your date and which check-in process applies to your passport.
- **Special cave visit.** After completing that day’s regular or emergency visit, bring your ID to the special cave desk in the cave area. Open caves and spaces depend on the day’s arrangements.

If you are coming for a particular mural, check with staff about access to that cave. You can study the [Nine-Colored Deer](/places/dunhuang/nine-colored-deer-meaning/) or the [Library Cave](/places/dunhuang/cave-17/) online before the trip.

## A long record of art and devotion

The Mogao Caves form a major complex of rock-cut Buddhist cave temples near Dunhuang, with murals, sculptures, and manuscripts connected to centuries of artistic and religious activity.

Conservation shapes the route through the caves. Follow the guide inside and keep cameras away while in the caves, in line with the [Academy’s visitor rules](https://www.dha.ac.cn/info/1020/7498.htm).

Digital Dunhuang offers a valuable way to study selected caves in detail before or after a visit. Continue with the [Dunhuang murals](/places/dunhuang/murals/), [Dunhuang manuscripts](/places/dunhuang/manuscripts/), and [Library Cave](/places/dunhuang/cave-17/).
